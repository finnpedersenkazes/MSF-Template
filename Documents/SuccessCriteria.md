# Success Criteria

When I show you this, you will pay me. Right?

- Lav et Demo Script

## Demo Script

*How do you intend to prove that you have delivered the solution to your customer?*


*The demo script is much more than its name indicates. It consists of real life examples that are used to design the solution, 
make decisions about the technology to use, how the developer should validate the solution, how to test the solution, and how to 
train the users, and finally how to prove to the paying customer that you have delivered what you promised.* 

## Test Setup
Det er vigtigt at dette afsluttes inden programmering starter, da det er en væsentlig del af valideringen af designet.

For at kunne teste løsningen fra Business Central og for at kunne teste designet ved hjælp af Postman, har vi brug for følgende test elementer fra lagerhotellet.  

- configuration
- company
- encryptionkey
- shopId

desuden vil vi skulle bruge konkrete eksempler på webservice operationer som vi kan sætte på i Postman. 

Hvis disse findes som Postman collections allerede vil det forenkle vores arbejde.
