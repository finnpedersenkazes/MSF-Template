## Demo Script

How do you intend to prove that you have delivered the solution to your customer?


The demo script is much more than its name indicates. It consists of real life examples that are used to design the solution, 
make decisions about the technology to use, how the developer should validate the solution, how to test the solution, and how to 
train the users, and finally how to prove to the paying customer that you have delivered what you promised.
